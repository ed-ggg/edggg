< GGG #66

Discovered By: CMDR jacob99

Date: 3312-08-26

Description:

Located only 14 LY from the border of Odin's Hold, this Class I GGG orbits its parent all by its lonesome. It has a good number of green streaks on its surface, though not very large. It mostly hosts dark red coloration on its poles and other random areas on its surface. There are K10-Type Anomalies present in the planet's stable Lagrange points. This planet might have been captured or disrupted—it has a fairly elliptical orbit, and has a very long orbital period exceeding 150,000 days, or 410 earth years. It is the only planet in the system.
