Description:

An amazing Green GGWBL with a dark red-maroon base cloud layer with a slight purple tint, and long flowing streaks of green clouds that wrap the planet. The poles are completely enveloped in green. It has no landable moons, but can barely be seen from the surface of the moons orbiting the other gas giant it is in binary with. This is the third GGG to be found searching archived data for matching temperatures. Rediscovered by CMDR Ozzie J. Isaacs & CMDR c1bra on 3312-08-29
